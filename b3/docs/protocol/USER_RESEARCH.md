# BCID User Research Program

Validate demand for BCID. Target: **100 interviews**, top 20 pain points, top 10 opportunities.

**Status:** Month 1 launch — program defined; interviews in progress.

---

## Research questions

| # | Question | Competitor frame |
|---|----------|------------------|
| 1 | Why would you use BCID? | vs doing nothing |
| 2 | Why not LinkedIn? | professional identity |
| 3 | Why not Farcaster? | social identity |
| 4 | What is painful today about proving who you are online? | open |
| 5 | Would you pay ~$1 for a portable identity? | pricing |
| 6 | What credentials matter most to you? | credential priority |
| 7 | Do you trust AI agents acting on your behalf? | agent BCID |
| 8 | What would make you bridge from `.culture`? | interop |

## Interview segments

| Segment | Target n | Recruitment |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Existing `.culture` holders | 30 | In-app prompt + email |
| Waitlist (no mint yet) | 25 | Waitlist outreach |
| Farcaster builders | 20 | Cast + Frame CTA |
| Grant applicants | 15 | Grant Proof users |
| Agent developers | 10 | Agent OS + x402 users |

**Total target:** 100 interviews by Month 2 end.

---

## Methodology

- 20-minute structured video calls (Cal.com link in waitlist convert email)
- Consent form + anonymized notes in Notion/research doc
- Incentive: 25 BCC for completed interview (cap 100)
- Synthesis weekly by Research Agent
