# Trust Layer — community & investor copy

## Product narrative

Building Culture is building the trust layer for the digital economy — a portable identity where humans, communities, businesses, and AI agents earn verifiable credentials, build reputation, unlock access, and participate in a chain-agnostic economy powered by BCC. Base is our live social and execution layer today; XRPL is an optional future rail for credentials and settlement—not a pivot, infrastructure under Culture ID.
