Abstract

Building Culture is a proof-first on-chain culture ecosystem on Base. It connects community participation, portable identity, utility token economics, AI agent accountability, and real-world asset (RWA) infrastructure into one inspectable stack. The platform ships culture receipts on-chain: verified identity (.culture names and BCID), reputation and credentials, BCC utility settlement, fractional property shares (Places REOC), provably fair experience drops, and agent identity on 0G.

Every major claim is verifiable in minutes via automated production checks — not narrative alone. This document describes the problem, architecture, token model, compliance posture, agent economy, roadmap, and risks. It is technical protocol material — not legal, tax, or investment advice.

1. Introduction

1.1 The Gap

Web3 culture and real-estate participation today suffer from four structural failures:

  1. No portable professional identity — Credentials live in silos. DAOs cannot cheaply verify shipped work versus social farming.
  2. High sybil cost — Human-proof layers exist but are not integrated with builder reputation.
  3. Undefined agent accountability — AI agents operate wallets without standard binding to human owners or revenue attribution.
  4. Opaque RWA settlement — Fractional participation lacks inspectable settlement and compliance hooks.

1.2 North Star

Build a proof-first on-chain culture ecosystem where community actions, identity, and utility are measurable, attributable, and compounding across products.

2. Problem Statement

2.1 Builder Identity Is Not Portable

Professional credentials are platform-bound. Web3 grants cannot verify that an applicant has shipped work. Follower counts are poor proxies for contribution.

2.2 Sybil Resistance Without Reputation Integration

World ID proves humanity but not professional contribution. EAS provides schemas without a canonical identity namespace.

2.3 Agent Economy Lacks Ownership Graphs

AI agents need discoverable identity (ERC-8004), delegated wallets with spend caps, verifiable task history, and revenue attribution.

2.4 RWA Needs Compliance-Native Infrastructure

Tokenized real estate requires permissioned transfers, NAV-priced subscription/redemption, reserve-backed mint caps, and KYC-to-onchain hooks.

4.2 BCID v1 (Parallel Identity Standard)

Building Culture Identity (BCID) is a soulbound, portable decentralized identifier complementing the live .culture trust layer.

Four reputation scores: Builder, Trust, Contribution, Verification. No follower weight. Recovery via 2-of-3 guardians with 72-hour timelock. Interoperates with ENS, EAS, World ID, ERC-8004, and .culture bridge.
