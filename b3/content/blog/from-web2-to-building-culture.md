Building Culture did not appear overnight. Our co-founder Laszlo Bihary started in IT in **1996** — agencies, SEO, creative direction, and later wallet-native products — before converging on a single question: *what if communities could fund and shape the places they care about, with receipts?*

> “Building Culture is what happens when you stop optimizing for launches and start optimizing for places people actually live in.”

That line is the spirit of the essay **From Web2 Builder to Building Culture**, published on [Paragraph](https://paragraph.com/0x502ce9fb1814cb03843967ec5e0d8f6aa3a3c2e1). We keep a curated chronicle in the app — excerpts and timeline — so newcomers meet the human arc before the ecosystem map.
