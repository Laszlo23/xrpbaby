## Follow along — three simple paths

### 1) “I’m new — where do I click?”

1. Open **[home.buildingcultureid.space](https://home.buildingcultureid.space/)** for the narrative and ecosystem map.  
2. When you’re ready to connect a wallet, open **[0x.buildingcultureid.space](https://0x.buildingcultureid.space/)** (or **[app.buildingcultureid.space](https://app.buildingcultureid.space/)**).  
3. Explore **Mission**, **drops**, **marketplace**, and **FAQ** inside the app footer.

### 2) “I care about places, hubs, and revival”

1. Visit **[eco.buildingcultureid.space](https://eco.buildingcultureid.space/)** for the eco storyline.  
2. Use **Marketplace ↗** from there when you want on-chain listings and tickets in the BUILDCHAIN app.

### 3) “I’m an investor or reader — not minting today”

1. **[buildingcultureid.space](https://buildingcultureid.space/)** — apex positioning.  
2. **[home.buildingcultureid.space](https://home.buildingcultureid.space/)** — broader ecosystem view.  
3. Still curious about product mechanics? Skim **[FAQ](https://0x.buildingcultureid.space/faq)** on the app.
